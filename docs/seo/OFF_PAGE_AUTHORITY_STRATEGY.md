## 4. Natural Anchor Text Strategy

Search engine algorithms heavily penalize unnatural or keyword-stuffed anchor text distributions. A resilient backlink profile must be predominantly branded and natural:

### Recommended Anchor Text Distribution:
1. **Branded Anchors (50% – 60%):**
   - `Rongin Libas`
   - `Rongin Libas Atelier`
   - `ronginlibas.com`
   - `Rongin Libas Dhaka`
2. **Natural & Topical Anchors (25% – 30%):**
   - `this Bangladeshi menswear brand`
   - `their mercerized polo collection`
   - `Rongin Libas imperial panjabis`
   - `men's clothing by Rongin Libas`
   - `the Rongin Libas atelier`
   - `sartorial guides on Rongin Libas`
3. **Resource / Citation Anchors (10% – 15%):**
   - `Rongin Libas clothing care guide`
   - `polo shirt size and fit guide`
   - `fabric guidelines for tropical weather`
   - `doorstep open-box inspection policy`
4. **Exact Commercial Anchors (< 5%):**
   - Keep exact-match commercial anchors (e.g., `buy panjabi online`) strictly minimal to protect against algorithmic link penalties.

## 6. Outreach Quality Rules & Anti-Spam Safeguards

To safeguard brand reputation and domain authority, all future outreach must adhere strictly to these principles:

### Permitted (People-First Practices):
- **Earned Editorial Coverage:** Pitching genuine stories to fashion editors and journalists.
- **Expert Citations:** Offering sartorial commentary on textile care, cotton staple lengths, and tropical sizing.
- **Unbiased Creator Reviews:** Providing sample garments for honest creator assessment without contractual link mandates.
- **Authentic Industry Directories:** Submitting verified business details to recognized Bangladesh commercial directories.
- **Organic Social Sharing:** Facilitating customer and patron sharing via native zero-script share tools.

### Strictly Prohibited (Search Manipulation Penalties):
- **No Paid Followed Backlinks:** Never purchase links disguised as editorial content.
- **No Private Blog Networks (PBNs):** Avoid automated, artificial content networks.
- **No Automated Link Insertion:** Never use spam scripts, comment bots, or automated forum signatures.
- **No Large-Scale Reciprocal Schemes:** Avoid "link to me and I link to you" circular pacts.
- **No Irrelevant Web Placements:** Do not acquire links from non-fashion, adult, gambling, or unrelated foreign-language domains.

## 7. Campaign Measurement & UTM Standards

When coordinating official press releases, influencer partnerships, or editorial campaigns, append standard UTM parameters to incoming URLs **without altering internal canonical tags**:

### Standard UTM Parameter Structure:
```
https://ronginlibas.com/[destination]?utm_source=[platform]&utm_medium=[channel]&utm_campaign=[campaign_name]
```

### Approved Campaign Conventions:
- **`utm_source`:** Name of publication or creator (e.g., `ice-today`, `daily-star`, `creator-name`).
- **`utm_medium`:** Channel type (e.g., `editorial`, `influencer`, `press-release`, `referral`).
- **`utm_campaign`:** Campaign identifier (e.g., `eid-ceremonial-2026`, `polo-launch`, `atelier-heritage`).

> **Note on SEO Safety:**
> - UTM parameters are strictly for external tracking.
> - They must **never** be used in internal links.
> - They must **never** be included in `sitemap.xml`.
> - The application's canonical tag ensures that URLs with UTM queries always resolve canonical authority to the clean target URL.

## 8. Authority Growth Monitoring KPIs

Monitor the following key metrics in **Google Search Console** and web analytics to assess off-page authority growth:

1. **Referring Domain Velocity:** Steady, natural month-over-month increase in unique referring domains.
2. **Brand Search Query Volume:** Growth in searches for `"Rongin Libas"`, `"Rongin Libas Panjabi"`, `"Rongin Libas Polos"`.
3. **Editorial Citation Ratio:** Percentage of backlinks originating from high-trust news, editorial, and educational platforms.
4. **Journal Organic Landing Pages:** Number of non-brand impressions and clicks captured by `/journal/*` articles.
5. **Assisted Conversion Path:** Assessing how informational journal visits contribute to subsequent commercial checkouts.
